This guide is not designed to convince you to quit your job.

It is designed to help you answer a more useful question:

What would need to be true for your next move to become a credible business, not simply an escape?

Take your time with it. Write in it. Challenge your assumptions. You do not need to have the perfect idea yet.

You need a better way to recognize the right one.

A small suggestion

Do not read the guide only once. Return to the three questions whenever a new idea appears. A promising business should become clearer under examination, not more dependent on enthusiasm.
